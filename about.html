How It All Started

A Passion for Baking,
A Dream of Sharing

Sweet Crumbs was born in 2018 from a simple belief: that every occasion deserves a cake as memorable as the moment itself. Our founder, Adaeze Okafor, began baking in her Lagos home kitchen for friends and family — pouring love and precision into every tier, every swirl of buttercream, every hand-painted detail.

Word spread quickly. Birthday parties, bridal showers, corporate events — orders flooded in. By 2020, Sweet Crumbs had outgrown the kitchen and moved into a dedicated bakery space in Victoria Island. Today, with a team of passionate bakers and cake artists, we serve hundreds of customers across Nigeria every month.

Our philosophy hasn't changed: every cake should be the most beautiful, most delicious thing at the table. Whether it's a simple birthday sponge or a six-tier wedding masterpiece, we pour the same passion into everything we make.
